# Multi-Brand-Go-to-Market-Strategy-STP-Analysis

This project demonstrates the application of structured market research and marketing strategy frameworks to business decision-making. It highlights the ability to assess consumer markets, evaluate brand positioning, and translate analytical insights into practical recommendations for growth and commercial competitiveness.
Hamleys: Toy retail, experiential shopping, and family-oriented consumer preferences.
Da Milano: Premium leather accessories, aspirational consumption, and value perception.
Taco Bell: Quick-service dining, customer preferences, and value-driven positioning
